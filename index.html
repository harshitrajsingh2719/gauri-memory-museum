My Person.

Gauri,

I don't think I ever properly understood what love meant before you became such an important part of my life.

We started as two people who simply knew each other. Then came conversations, rainy days, the café, cold coffee, countless chats and all those ordinary moments that slowly became important.

I still remember how nervous I was when I finally tried to say what I was feeling on that late July night.

What I value most isn't one particular thing about you. It's your presence, your understanding and the way you have been there through different parts of my life.

We aren't perfect. We have disagreements. Sometimes we misunderstand each other. Sometimes we say things we regret. But we also come back, talk, apologize, forgive, laugh again and keep learning.

And maybe that's what makes these memories worth keeping.

Thank you for being part of my story.
